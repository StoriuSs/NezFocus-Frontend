# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 01/11/2026

This policy explains what data NezFocus collects and how it is used.

## 1. What we collect

- **Account information:** email, username, display name, password (hashed), avatar, bio.
- **What you create:** messages, attachments, servers, group tasks.
- **Study data:** study sessions, study time, streaks.
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser type, time of access.
- **When you sign in with Google:** only your email, your name and your Google account's identifier; we do not take your profile picture or store Google's access tokens.

Voice, video and screen sharing are never recorded.

## 2. Why we use it

- To provide and keep up the features of NezFocus.
- To protect accounts and prevent abuse.
- To send the emails you need, such as sign-in codes and password resets.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Sharing

- Your profile and what you post are shown to other people the way you share them (servers, channels, direct messages). Your email is not shown to anyone else.
- We use a few third-party services to run NezFocus (servers, file storage, email delivery, voice and video).
- We give data to public authorities only when the law requires it.

## 4. Cookies

NezFocus uses a cookie to keep you signed in, and the browser's storage to remember a few preferences. We do not use advertising cookies.

## 5. Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data is deleted after 14 days; messages you sent may still show under the name "Deleted account".

## 6. Your rights

You can see and edit your personal information in Settings, turn off sharing your study activity, or delete your account at any time.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable security measures, such as encrypted connections and hashed passwords, but no system is perfectly safe.

## 8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The date of the latest update is at the top of the page.
